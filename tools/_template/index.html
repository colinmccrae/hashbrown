Assumptions & sources
  • Say where the numbers came from. Name the standard, the publication or the library, and say whether they were checked against it or merely copied from it.
  • Say what is assumed. Every tool here has at least one figure that is a choice rather than a constant — a reference condition, a density, an atmosphere. State it, and make it selectable if a reasonable person would choose differently.
  • Say what is not modelled. This is the most useful section and the easiest to skip. A reader who knows what the tool ignores can decide whether to trust it.
  • Say what would surprise someone. If two things that look identical are not, or two standards round the same dimension differently, that belongs here.

A reference tool, not a design document. Check the assumptions above against whatever actually governs your work.
